香菜柠檬鸡
📕 小红书 · Jrake
鸡腿肉 · 香菜 · 柠檬 · 蘑菇

材料

鸡腿肉

配料

香菜

柠檬

蘑菇（海鲜菇）

步骤

  1. 鸡腿围着碗摆放。
  2. 在蒸锅中大火蒸20分钟（蒸箱约25分钟），从水滚上汽后开始计时。

制作过程

  1. 葱白切段，锅中加入大量底油，中小火慢煎葱白。
  2. 柠檬一半切片，一半挤汁。
  3. 准备香菜末、蒜末和小米辣。
  4. 蒸好的鸡腿放入冰水，然后手撕成块。
  5. 将蒸鸡的鸡汤倒入葱油中，加入海鲜菇，小火收汁。
  6. 在大盆中加入鸡肉、大蒜、小米辣、香菜，淋上柠檬汁。
  7. 加入生抽、香油、一勺白砂糖，倒入葱油蘑菇鸡汤，拌匀。

提示和建议

  • 蒸鸡的时间根据设备调整，确保鸡肉熟透且嫩滑。
  • 使用初榨橄榄油可能会影响菜肴的口感，建议选择一般的食用油。
  • 拌匀鸡肉时，最好用手抓拌，这样更入味。
  • 准备时间：20分钟 · 烹饪时间：40分钟（包括蒸鸡和后续烹饪）

🐟Cajun 海鲜 (Hot n Juicy style)
shrimp · snow crab legs · andouille sausage · baby potatoes · corn · Cajun garlic butter

Serves 3–4. Order: potatoes → corn → sausage → Cajun garlic-butter sauce → potatoes → corn → sausage → crab → shrimp → lemon

Seafood & sausage

  • 1.5 lb shrimp, peeled & deveined
  • 1.5–2 lb cooked snow crab legs
  • 12 oz andouille sausage (or hot Italian pork sausage)

Sauce

  • 6 Tbsp butter · 1 Tbsp neutral oil
  • 8–10 garlic cloves, finely minced
  • 1–2 tsp sugar · 1–2 tsp Louisiana-style hot sauce
  • 1/4 cup water or chicken stock (up to 1/2 cup, only if needed)

Seasoning

  • 1 Tbsp each: smoked paprika, Old Bay, Cajun seasoning, lemon pepper
  • 1/2 tsp cayenne (to start) · 1 tsp dried thyme
  • For the boiling water: 1–2 Tbsp salt, optional 1 Tbsp Old Bay

Sides

  • 1.5 lb baby potatoes (halve the larger ones)
  • 3–4 ears corn, each cut into 2–3 pieces
  • 1 lemon · optional parsley · optional rice or crusty bread

Salt: Old Bay, Cajun seasoning, sausage and crab are already salty. Don't salt the sauce until you taste at the very end.

Steps

  1. Prep: peel/devein the shrimp, pat very dry, keep cold (don't pre-cook). Keep the crab cold; if frozen, thaw in the fridge, rinse off the ice glaze and drain (don't boil it again). Slice the sausage ~1/2 inch thick. Mince the garlic and measure every spice beside the stove.
  2. Potatoes: cover with cold water by ~1 inch, add 1–2 Tbsp salt (+ optional 1 Tbsp Old Bay). Boil, then simmer 12–15 min until a knife goes in with slight resistance (80–90% cooked). Lift out; keep the water.
  3. Corn: bring the water back to a boil, cook the corn 3–5 min, drain. Discard the water.
  4. Sausage: large deep skillet / Dutch oven, medium-high, no oil. Fully cooked andouille: brown 3–5 min. Raw pork sausage: brown, then cook until the center reaches 160°F / 71°C. Remove; leave the fat and browned bits in the pan.
  5. Sauce: medium heat. Add up to 1 Tbsp oil only if there is little fat. Melt 6 Tbsp butter (don't let it brown). Add the garlic and stir 30–60 s until fragrant but still pale.
  6. Add all the spices and stir ~30 s to bloom. Stir in the sugar and hot sauce. Add water/stock 2–4 Tbsp at a time until glossy (about 1/4 cup total, no more than ~1/2 cup).
  7. Potatoes back in: toss to coat, cook 3–4 min over medium, turning every 30–60 s.
  8. Corn: turn through the sauce, cook 2–3 min.
  9. Sausage back: toss ~1 min to reheat and coat.
  10. Crab: lay it on top (separate at the joints if too big). If the pan looks dry, add 2–4 Tbsp water/stock around the edge. Cover 2 min, turn the crab with tongs, cover 1–2 min more until steaming hot. Reheat only, don't boil.
  11. Shrimp last: push things to the sides, add the dry shrimp in one layer. 1–2 min per side; turn off the heat as soon as they're opaque (loose C shape; a tight curl means overcooked).
  12. Finish off the heat: squeeze in 1/2 lemon, toss, taste a potato. More heat → hot sauce or a pinch of cayenne; brighter → more lemon; too thick → 1–2 Tbsp hot water/stock; too thin → take the shrimp out, reduce briefly. Salt last, just a pinch. Optional: stir in 1 Tbsp cold butter for a richer, shinier sauce.
  13. Serve right away: spoon all the pan sauce over everything; lemon wedges and parsley; rice or crusty bread on the side.

Sauce texture: rich and glossy, coats everything. Not soupy, no pool of thin liquid at the bottom.

Food safety: keep raw shrimp away from cooked food and utensils. Raw pork sausage to 160°F / 71°C; reheat leftovers to 165°F / 74°C.

🍰🍅 意大利番茄佛卡夏
高筋面粉 · 小番茄 · 橄榄油 · 迷迭香
⏱ 当天版 · 450°F

配方适用于：室温 72°F（22°C）· 烤盘 280 × 280 × 36 mm · 面粉 Bread Flour（高筋面粉）· 酵母 Fleischmann's Active Dry Yeast

材料 · 面团

  • Bread Flour（高筋面粉）500g
  • 水 400g（50g 用于激活酵母，350g 用于和面）
  • Fleischmann's Active Dry Yeast 3g
  • 盐 10g
  • 特级初榨橄榄油 30g

材料 · 烤盘

  • 橄榄油 2～3 大勺

材料 · 表面

  • Cherry Tomatoes 小番茄 200～250g
  • 特级初榨橄榄油 2 大勺
  • Maldon 或粗海盐
  • 新鲜迷迭香（推荐）
  • （可选）牛至
  • （可选）薄蒜片

第一步：激活酵母

  • 准备：温水 50g + Active Dry Yeast 3g + 一小撮糖（可选）
  • 水温：35～40°C（95～105°F），静置 5～10 分钟
  • 判断标准：表面出现泡沫、有明显酵母香，即可开始下一步。

第二步：混合面团

  • 加入：Bread Flour 500g、剩余350g水、激活好的酵母液
  • 混合到 完全没有干粉即可。不用揉，不用揉出膜。盖保鲜膜。

第三步：自溶（Autolyse）

  • 静置 30分钟
  • 为什么要自溶？让面粉充分吸水、自动形成面筋、后面更容易操作高含水面团
  • 30分钟后你会发现：面团更光滑、黏性降低一点、拉起来比较有弹性

第四步：加入盐和橄榄油

  • 加入：盐 10g、橄榄油 30g
  • 用湿手：抓、捏、折，约2～4分钟。
  • 刚开始会觉得很滑、像散掉一样，这是正常现象。继续抓拌，最后会重新变成一整团。盖好。

第一发酵（Bulk Fermentation）

  • 室温 72°F（22°C）
  • 第一次折叠（30分钟后）：Stretch & Fold，四边往中间折。盖好。
  • 第二～四次折叠（每30分钟）：Coil Fold，双手从中间托起，让两端自然折到下面；转90°，重复一次。盖好。
  • 第四次折叠完成以后继续发酵 30～60分钟，不要只看时间。
  • 完成标准：✅ 面团增加约50～70% ✅ 表面开始出现很多小气泡 ✅ 面团摸起来很轻 ✅ 摇晃容器时有轻微果冻感。不用等翻倍。

入盘 · 松弛 · 拉伸

  • 烤盘抹 2～3大勺橄榄油，底部和四周全部抹到。轻轻把面团倒进去，不要揉，不要排气。
  • 盖保鲜膜，静置30分钟。
  • 如果面团没有铺满：轻轻拉向四个角。如果已经铺满：不用拉。

第二次发酵（Final Proof）

  • 盖保鲜膜，室温72°F，约 1.5～2小时，不要只看时间。
  • 完成标准：✅ 面团明显鼓起 ✅ 表面很多气泡 ✅ 手指轻按缓慢回弹 ✅ 摇晃烤盘像充气床垫

预热烤箱

  • 第二发酵最后30分钟开始，450°F（230°C），至少预热30分钟。烤架：中下层。

处理番茄

  • 小番茄 200～250g，切半。
  • 喜欢番茄味更浓：可以把一半番茄 375°F 烤20分钟，另一半保持新鲜，混合使用风味最好。

最后整形

  1. 淋橄榄油
  2. 手指压洞（压到底）
  3. 放番茄
  4. 撒粗海盐
  5. 撒迷迭香

不要把番茄全部埋进去，轻轻放在凹洞即可。

烘烤

  • 450°F（230°C），先烤20分钟，不要开门。20分钟开始检查，通常 22～25分钟完成。
  • 烤好标准：✅ 表面深金黄色 ✅ 边缘焦糖色 ✅ 番茄边缘轻微焦化
  • 如果底部颜色不够：脱模，直接放烤架，再烤3～5分钟。

出炉 · 食用

  • 立即放到烤架，不要一直留在烤盘。趁热淋一点优质橄榄油，撒一点 Maldon 海盐。
  • 等待15～20分钟再切。最佳食用温度：温热（Warm）
  • 搭配推荐：Burrata · 帕尔玛火腿（Prosciutto）· 一小碟优质橄榄油 · 帕玛森奶酪

成功关键（一定要记住）

  • ✅ 自溶30分钟不要省
  • ✅ 第一发酵不要死看时间，看面团状态
  • ✅ 第一轮 Stretch & Fold，后三轮 Coil Fold
  • ✅ 全程盖保鲜膜，避免表面变干
  • ✅ 不要排气
  • ✅ 先淋油，再压洞，再放番茄
  • ✅ 烤箱一定充分预热30分钟
  • ✅ 烤到深金黄色，不要怕颜色深
  • ✅ 出炉放凉15～20分钟再切

完整指南 Full guide

If you enjoy the tender and juicy texture of the chicken you purchased, you can easily replicate the process at home using natural methods like brining and marinating. Here's a step-by-step guide to help you achieve similar results:

1. Brining (Water + Salt Solution)

Brining is one of the best ways to ensure your chicken stays moist and tender. Here's how you can do it:

Basic Brine Recipe · Ingredients

  • 4 cups (1 liter) of water
  • 1/4 cup kosher salt (or 3 tablespoons table salt)
  • 2 tablespoons sugar (optional, helps with caramelization and adds flavor)
  • Optional: add flavorings such as lemon juice, garlic cloves, bay leaves, peppercorns, or herbs (like thyme or rosemary)

Instructions

  1. Dissolve salt and sugar: In a saucepan, heat 1 cup of the water and dissolve the salt and sugar. If you're using additional flavorings, add them now. Once dissolved, remove from heat and let it cool.
  2. Add cold water: Once the brine base is cool, add the remaining 3 cups of cold water to cool the mixture completely. You don't want to add chicken to a warm brine, as it could start cooking it.
  3. Brine the chicken: Submerge the chicken in the brine solution. You can brine for 30 minutes to 12 hours, depending on how much time you have and how strong you want the brine to be. A good rule of thumb: 30 minutes to 2 hours for smaller cuts like breasts or thighs; 4-12 hours for whole chickens or larger pieces.
  4. Rinse and dry: After brining, remove the chicken and rinse it briefly under cold water to remove excess salt. Pat it dry with paper towels before cooking.

Why it works: The salt in the brine helps the chicken retain moisture, keeping it juicy during cooking. It also breaks down proteins slightly, tenderizing the meat. Sugar adds a touch of sweetness and helps with browning.

2. Marinating (Acid + Oil + Seasonings)

If you want to further enhance the flavor and texture, you can marinate the chicken. A marinade typically includes an acid (like lemon juice or vinegar), oil, and seasonings. The acid helps tenderize the meat, while the oil keeps it moist.

Basic Marinade Recipe · Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 2 tablespoons lemon juice (or any acid like vinegar)
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon pepper
  • Optional: herbs (rosemary, thyme, or oregano), honey for sweetness, or soy sauce for umami

Instructions

  1. Mix ingredients: In a small bowl, whisk together the oil, lemon juice, garlic, salt, pepper, and any additional herbs or flavorings.
  2. Marinate the chicken: Place the chicken in a resealable plastic bag or a shallow dish. Pour the marinade over the chicken, ensuring it's evenly coated. Refrigerate and marinate for 30 minutes to 4 hours.
  3. Cook as desired: Remove the chicken from the marinade, letting any excess drip off, and cook using your preferred method (grilling, baking, pan-searing, etc.).

Why it works: The acid (lemon juice) helps break down the proteins on the surface of the chicken, tenderizing it. The oil helps keep the chicken moist, and the garlic and herbs add flavor.

3. Tenderizing Alternatives (Optional)

If you want to replicate the tenderizing effects of sodium carbonate (used in commercial tenderizing), you can use baking soda or natural tenderizers like buttermilk:

Baking Soda Method — Baking soda raises the pH of the meat, making it harder for proteins to bond together, which keeps the meat tender and moist.

  1. Dissolve 1 teaspoon of baking soda in 1 cup of water.
  2. Submerge the chicken in the solution for 15-20 minutes (no longer, or the texture may become too soft).
  3. Rinse thoroughly and pat dry before cooking.

Buttermilk Method — Buttermilk's mild acidity also helps tenderize chicken while adding a slight tangy flavor.

  1. Submerge the chicken in buttermilk for 2 to 12 hours.
  2. Remove from the buttermilk, shake off the excess, and cook.

4. Cooking Methods to Retain Moisture

Regardless of how you prepare your chicken, cooking it properly is key to maintaining its tender texture. Here are a few cooking tips:

  • Use moderate heat: Cook chicken over medium heat to avoid drying it out too quickly.
  • Monitor internal temperature: Chicken is fully cooked when it reaches an internal temperature of 165°F (74°C). Overcooking can make it dry, so use a meat thermometer for precision.
  • Rest the meat: Let the chicken rest for a few minutes after cooking to allow the juices to redistribute, keeping it moist.

Conclusion

By brining and/or marinating your chicken at home, you can easily achieve the tender, juicy texture you enjoy from store-bought chicken. Start with a simple saltwater brine, and you can add herbs or acids like lemon juice to further tenderize and flavor the meat. If you're looking for a more advanced tenderizing method, you can experiment with baking soda or buttermilk. With these steps, you can have consistently tender and flavorful chicken just like the kind you'd buy from the store!
